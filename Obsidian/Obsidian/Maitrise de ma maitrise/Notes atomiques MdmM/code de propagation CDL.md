- « This code utilizes a parabolic approximation to the convected Helmholtz equation and offers a computationally efficient model that accounts for the complexities of fully three-dimensional nacelle configurations. » (Nark et Jones, 2021, p. 443)

- « the CDL code supports multiblock propagation calculations in which grid connectivity is determined and data is transferred from upstream to downstream blocks without user intervention. » (Nark et Jones, 2021, p. 443)

- « The duct radiation model is based on the Ffowcs Williams-Hawkings (FW-H) equation with a permeable data surface. » (Nark et Jones, 2021, p. 443)

- « provisions have also been made to account for the effects of the shear layer on the radiation. » (Nark et Jones, 2021, p. 443)
