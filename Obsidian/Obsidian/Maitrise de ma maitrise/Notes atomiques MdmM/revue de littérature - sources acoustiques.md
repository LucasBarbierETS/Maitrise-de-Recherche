- « Further analysis of the sources of rotor acoustics can be found in Chapter 4 of the book by Goldstein [11] and the review papers by Morfey [12], Wright [13], and Huff [14]. » (Tomko et al., 2019, p. 85)

- « The radiated sound generated by a rotating blade row can be considered to be a combination of both the “acoustic sources” and an “acoustic transfer function”. In many applications the acoustic source can be considered to be a dipole-like sound mechanism that results from the interaction of rotating blades with a turbulent approach flow [15]. » (Tomko et al., 2019, p. 85)
