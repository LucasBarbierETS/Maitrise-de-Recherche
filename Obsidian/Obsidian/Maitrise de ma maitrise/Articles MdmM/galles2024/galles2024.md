	- An alternative approach, developed by the second author, is the “direct optimization method,” in which liner parameters are directly optimized to maximize attenuation instead of indirectly optimizing sound attenuation by attempting to match a target acoustic impedance spectrum. (page [3](ann.desktopURI))                             
	- In the first step, the target impedance spectrum is determined based on application-specific objectives (page [5](ann.desktopURI))               
	- Attempting to maximize overall sound attenuation resulted in large single frequency notches in sound attenuation that led to poor broadband attenuation performance (page [6](ann.desktopURI))     
	- As the power is increased from one toward infinity, the cost function places a greater emphasis on the maximum difference between the levels rather than on the average difference between levels. (page [6](ann.desktopURI))                   
	- Although the facesheet model (i.e., the MKM) is sensitive to grazing flow and chosen empirical constants such as the discharge coefficient (see Ref. [19, p. 170]), the SIM sufficiently captures key features of interest for this study. (page [10](ann.desktopURI))   
	- the direct optimization method produced liners with substantially different impedances depending on the frequency weighting used (page [13](ann.desktopURI))   
	- That liners designed using the direct method did not achieve the same attenuation as the indirect method liners suggests that improvements can be made to the target objective function. (page [13](ann.desktopURI))   
	- Performance assessment predictions from the first step of the indirect method could be used to inform the direct method objective function. (page [13](ann.desktopURI))   
	- the target attenuations in the direct method objective function could be weighted by predicted indirect method attenuations so that realizable attenuations at each of the target frequencies are considered. (page [13](ann.desktopURI))   
	- a penalty function could be incorporated in the objective function that would place additional penalty (increase the cost) on frequencies in which the target attenuation is not met. (page [13](ann.desktopURI))   
	- Alternatively, it is possible that the optimization method used (a gradient based method) found a local minimum, which could result in a design that may not be ideal. (page [13](ann.desktopURI))   
	- Both the indirect and direct methods converged on solutions with four unique cavity lengths; only the direct low frequency design has five unique cavity lengths. (page [14](ann.desktopURI))   
	- It could also be possible that the additional degree of freedom was not required by the optimizer. (page [14](ann.desktopURI))   
	- Thus, targeting reactance could result in a better performing liner. (page [14](ann.desktopURI))   
	- The impedance of each cavity could be affected by neighboring cavities in a variable depth liner. It is hypothesized that these effects could be due to phasing differences as sound waves travel different lengths in each cavity. (page [14](ann.desktopURI))   
	- Better results could possibly be achieved by targeting one-third octave band center frequencies within the frequency range of interest. (page [14](ann.desktopURI))
