
**Transmission Loss à partir de la formulation de la matrice de transfert**


>[!quote|yellow]+ Convention Pression-Vitesse ([page. 27](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/GNXTFEBQ?page=27&annotation=L929ZIHP))
>$$
TL = 20 \log \left( \frac{T_{11} + \frac{1}{\rho_0 c} T_{12} + \rho_0 c T_{21} + T_{22}}{2} \right)
$$

>[!quote|yellow]+ Convention Pression-Débit
>$$
>TL = 20 \log \left( \frac{T_{11} + \frac{S}{\rho_0 c} T_{12}^{(q)} + \frac{\rho_0 c}{S} T_{21}^{(q)} + T_{22}}{2} \right)
$$
