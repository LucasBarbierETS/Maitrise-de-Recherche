
**Approche empirique** 

> [!quote|green]+ [[Nombre de Strouhal]] ([page. 140](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/CMZQ7B9B?page=140&annotation=HSF5I7AD))
> Kooi and Sarin analysed by an experimental  investigation the air flow effect on the acoustic impedance of locally reacting perforated  liner for different boundary velocity distributions. They suggested orifice impedance as function of the inverse Strouhal number associated with the perforation diameter or plate  thickness. 

> [!quote|green]+ [[Coefficient de décharge]] ([page. 141](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/CMZQ7B9B?page=141&annotation=SV9682Z3))
> Hersh et al. developed a semi-empirical fluid mechanical model to predict the acoustic behavior  of single orifice Helmholtz resonator in the presence of grazing flow. They noted that the  incident sound field is connected to the cavity sound field by an orifice discharge coefficient which is obtained experimentally. They found that at high grazing flow  velocity, the acoustic resistance of the orifice is almost linearly proportional to the  grazing flow velocity whereas the reactance of the orifice is much smaller and tends  towards zero at high grazing flow velocity. They also observed that the reactance is quit  dependent upon orifice spacing and interactions between perforations become important  when the perforations are aligned parallel to the grazing flow.
