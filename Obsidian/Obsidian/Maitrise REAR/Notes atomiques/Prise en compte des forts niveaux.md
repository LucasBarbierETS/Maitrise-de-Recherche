**Approche empirique**

> [!quote|green]+ Formation de jet turbulent ([page. 70](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/CMZQ7B9B?page=70&annotation=RHL767VZ))
> The high excitation pressure  involves nonlinear phenomena which change also the reactance of the perforated panel. It  was suggested by Ingard and Ising that nonlinear orifice jetting reduces orifice end  correction. Furthermore, experimental measurements in reference show that as the SPL is increased, the end correction approaches one-half of its low amplitude value. This is due to the turbulent jet formation at the exit of the orifice.
