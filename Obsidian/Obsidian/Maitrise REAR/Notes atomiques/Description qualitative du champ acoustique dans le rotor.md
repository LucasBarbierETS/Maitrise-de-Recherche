> [!quote|green]+ Highlight ([page. 7](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/UDCIT46P?page=7&annotation=XKVUNL83))
> Khorrami et al., [4], identified two mechanisms responsible for tip clearance noise. One is due to the unsteady flow field in the tip vicinity interacting with the tip surface and producing self-generated broadband noise. 

> [!quote|green]+ Highlight ([page. 7](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/UDCIT46P?page=7&annotation=48U2BUK8))
> The second mechanism is due to the down-stream convection of the tip vortex interacting then with the stator vanes leading to mainly tonal noise.
