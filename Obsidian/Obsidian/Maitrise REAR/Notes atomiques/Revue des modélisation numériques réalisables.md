
### Logiciels disponibles

#### Actran

> [!quote|green]+ Highlight ([page. 533](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/KT3X6N29?page=533&annotation=YCFN67ZG))
> The commercially available FE acoustic prediction software Actran has been incorporated into this liner optimization processes. 

> [!quote|green]+ Highlight ([page. 533](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/KT3X6N29?page=533&annotation=QKQEL7F2))
> The solution methodology has proven quite reliable in estimating noise propagation and attenuation in complex lined ducts in the presence of realistic grazing flows and offers approximations that favor computational time while still providing accurate results. 

> [!quote|green]+ Highlight ([page. 533](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/KT3X6N29?page=533&annotation=HT4IGUQ8))
> An Actran model generally requires the creation of a computational mesh, a realistic CFD flow database, and the assembly of a finite element input file to specify the noise excitation and boundary conditions.
