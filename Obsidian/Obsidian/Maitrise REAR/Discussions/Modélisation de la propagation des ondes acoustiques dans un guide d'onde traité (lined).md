> The modeling of acoustic sound propagation in a lined duct is an important topic. It has been studied by many authors, leading to elaboration of analytical and numerical approaches to predict the sound transmission of the lined section. Different methods were developed to study this problem such as the multi-modal propagation method and perturbation methods. In particular, in the case of no flow, Munjal presented an analytical expression based on a fourth order Taylor series expansion of cotangent function to predict the propagating wave number in the duct. Wang and Sun presented a segmentation approach to study the sound transmission in a non-uniform duct with mean flow and Tsuji et al. used a finite element (FE) method to model the acoustic wave transmission in mean flow conditions. Nennig et al. proposed a mode matching method to predict the transmission loss of a dissipative silencer lined with a poroelastic foam in uniform flow environment.
