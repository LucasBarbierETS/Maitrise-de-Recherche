# (Z) Développement d’un code de simulation acoustique et d’optimisation de silencieux réactifs pour cheminées industrielles

> [!ABSTRACT]- ABSTRACT
>  
> Industrial chimneys are noise sources, which may produce discomfort in noise sensitive areas near industrial zones. In respond to this problem, reactive mufflers are used to reduce the noise of industrial chimneys before the sound spreads in the environment. Attenuation using resonators was studied, especially in the case of periodic structures. In use of transfer matrix method, the main goal is to model and optimize mufflers for industrial chimneys in order to decrease noise level (for low frequencies). To help the engineers, programs of modelling and optimization were designed to facilitate the sizing of the examined mufflers.
