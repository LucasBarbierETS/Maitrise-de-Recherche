> Hersh et al.  [61] developed a semi-empirical fluid mechanical model to predict the acoustic behavior  of single orifice Helmholtz resonator in the presence of grazing flow. They noted that the  incident sound field is connected to the cavity sound field by an orifice discharge  coefficient which is obtained experimentally. They also observed that the reactance is quit  dependent upon orifice spacing and interactions between perforations become important  when the perforations are aligned parallel to the grazing flow.
