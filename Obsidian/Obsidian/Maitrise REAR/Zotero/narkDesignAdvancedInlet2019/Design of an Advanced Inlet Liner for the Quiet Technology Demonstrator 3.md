### ✅ Supporting Argument or Example

> [!quote|green]+ Highlight ([page. 1](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/R73R9NG2?page=1&annotation=9GA97GQJ))
> an advanced inlet liner was designed for a Boeing 737MAX-7 to reduce drag and to improve the broadband noise reduction relative to conventional liners in use today 

> [!quote|green]+ Highlight ([page. 3](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/R73R9NG2?page=3&annotation=R586DGFP))
> Further discussion of the flight test campaign and data analysis are provided in companion papers 

> [!quote|green]+ Highlight ([page. 3](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/R73R9NG2?page=3&annotation=P4G925NA))
> duct propagation and radiation predictions were conducted using the CDUCT-LaRC (CDL) code 

> [!quote|green]+ Highlight ([page. 3](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/R73R9NG2?page=3&annotation=57CBK56X))
> CDUCT-LaRC 

> [!quote|green]+ Highlight ([page. 3](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/R73R9NG2?page=3&annotation=Y2AYJ4RC))
> This code calculates the propagation of a given acoustic source ahead of the fan face or aft of the exhaust guide vanes in the inlet or exhaust ducts, respectively 

> [!quote|green]+ Highlight ([page. 3](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/R73R9NG2?page=3&annotation=IKCHFMG2))
> Results of the propagation module include the acoustic potential or pressure within the duct 

> [!quote|green]+ Highlight ([page. 3](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/R73R9NG2?page=3&annotation=2NP2FQ3G))
> the data surface is taken to be the nacelle inlet or exhaust plane for inlet or aft-fan cases, respectively 

> [!quote|green]+ Highlight ([page. 3](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/R73R9NG2?page=3&annotation=4J2CD75R))
> it is convenient to specify the acoustic source distribution in terms of duct modes 

> [!quote|green]+ Highlight ([page. 3](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/R73R9NG2?page=3&annotation=N8GWA7ZP))
> transmitted power appears to be normally distributed if the source includes all propagating circumferential and radial acoustic modes with random modal power and phase 

> [!quote|green]+ Highlight ([page. 3](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/R73R9NG2?page=3&annotation=29MFSZ9B))
> As with previous liner optimization studies,1,3,5 in-duct attenuation was chosen as the cost function for the impedance optimization 

> [!quote|green]+ Highlight ([page. 4](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/R73R9NG2?page=4&annotation=S94CQT63))
> The impedance prediction model used in this design combines two models presented in an earlier paper.21 The first is a transmission line model22 that assumes acoustic wave propagation through each layer of the liner (see Fig. 2), and the second is a lumped element model23–25 used to compute the impedance change across perforates. The normalized surface impedance spectra presented by each chamber of the liner are computed separately, and are then combined to determine an effective surface impedance spectrum that is assumed uniform across the liner surface 

> [!quote|green]+ Highlight ([page. 5](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/R73R9NG2?page=5&annotation=VYKCYZVD))
> the CDL propagation code, coupled with optimization routines, was used to determine optimum impedance values, ζopt ( fi), at selected frequencies, fi, and flight conditions 

> [!quote|green]+ Highlight ([page. 5](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/R73R9NG2?page=5&annotation=LZXS4MNZ))
> 0 < θ ≤ 5 and −10 ≤ χ ≤ 10 

> [!quote|green]+ Highlight ([page. 5](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/R73R9NG2?page=5&annotation=CQEA2SCX))
> flow conditions representative of approach, cutback, and takeoff flight conditions 

> [!quote|green]+ Highlight ([page. 6](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/R73R9NG2?page=6&annotation=2TNYIFK9))
> candidate liner designs consisted of multilayer configurations incorporating septa (or “mesh-caps”) embedded into a honeycomb core 

> [!quote|green]+ Highlight ([page. 6](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/R73R9NG2?page=6&annotation=4L3BHP9M))
> manufacturing constraints (e.g., liner geometric parameters such as porosity and core depth) are key ingredients in this modeling phase, and must be taken into account to design acoustic liners that can be realistically achieved 

> [!quote|green]+ Highlight ([page. 6](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/R73R9NG2?page=6&annotation=TX2Q8RQD))
> approach, cutback, and takeoff conditions given weighting factors of W = 0.3, 0.5, and 1.0, respectively 

> [!quote|green]+ Highlight ([page. 6](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/R73R9NG2?page=6&annotation=M2K2UNU5))
> Further details on the facesheet manufacturing process are provided in a companion paper by Wong 

> [!quote|green]+ Highlight ([page. 7](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/R73R9NG2?page=7&annotation=FMPI275A))
> predictions at one-third octave band center frequencies from 400 Hz to 10000 Hz were carried out to obtain both in-duct and farfield levels 

> [!quote|green]+ Highlight ([page. 7](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/R73R9NG2?page=7&annotation=VGLPA2PR))
> a statistical source model was employed in which the amplitude and phase of each source mode were randomly selected over a number of trials using a uniform distribution 

> [!quote|green]+ Highlight ([page. 7](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/R73R9NG2?page=7&annotation=EGCTU8UZ))
> For each frequency and power setting, 11 simulations were performed to produce a sample population from which statistical information could be inferred 

> [!quote|green]+ Highlight ([page. 7](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/R73R9NG2?page=7&annotation=N68ERIGK))
> Farfield acoustic predictions were also obtained on a hemisphere (150 feet radius) centered on the engine inlet using the CDL radiation module 

> [!quote|green]+ Highlight ([page. 8](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/R73R9NG2?page=8&annotation=VD59Z7AH))
> flight test results showed the 3DOF liner to provide a 3.2 EPNdB cumulative inlet component benefit and a 0.7 EPNdB cumulative airplane benefit over the production liner
