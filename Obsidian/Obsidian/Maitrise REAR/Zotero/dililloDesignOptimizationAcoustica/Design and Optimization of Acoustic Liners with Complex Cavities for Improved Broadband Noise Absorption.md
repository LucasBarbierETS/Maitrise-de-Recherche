# (Z) Design and Optimization of Acoustic Liners with Complex Cavities for Improved Broadband Noise Absorption

> [!ABSTRACT]- ABSTRACT
>  
> The recent advances in aircraft propulsion systems and in sound-absorbing technologies has caused broadband fan noise to become more prominent and the engine noise spectrum to shift towards lower frequencies. However, this evolving noise source cannot be attenuated adequately by the conventional acoustic treatments installed in aircraft nacelles. While traditional designs are indeed effective at reducing narrowband noise sources, the sound absorption at low frequencies is limited due to space and weight restrictions.
