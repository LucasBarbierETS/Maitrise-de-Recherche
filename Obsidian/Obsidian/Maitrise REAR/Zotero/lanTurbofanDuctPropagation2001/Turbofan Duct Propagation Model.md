### ✅ Supporting Argument or Example

> [!quote|green]+ Highlight ([page. 2](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/Z37C5XV8?page=2&annotation=WE7FQYTQ))
> The CDUCT code is based on a parabolic approximation to the convected Helmholtz equation in an orthogonal curvilinear coordinate system 

> [!quote|green]+ Highlight ([page. 5](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/Z37C5XV8?page=5&annotation=BQ576NN4))
> The transverse boundary conditions of the duct are assumed to be locally reacting impedance surfaces 

> [!quote|green]+ Highlight ([page. 6](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/Z37C5XV8?page=6&annotation=9NBBXTVT))
> The initial acoustic velocity potential that describes the noise source at the entrance of the duct must be specified 

> [!quote|green]+ Highlight ([page. 27](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/Z37C5XV8?page=27&annotation=TGCXML6D))
> The CDUCT code can optimize the wall impedance to maximize the acoustic attenuation at all frequencies of interest 

> [!quote|green]+ Highlight ([page. 27](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/Z37C5XV8?page=27&annotation=V4UDK772))
> The objective is to maximize the noise power attenuation for a given frequency, duct geometry, flow, and noise source by optimizing the wall lining impedance. 

> [!quote|green]+ Highlight ([page. 27](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/Z37C5XV8?page=27&annotation=GUVQGX7A))
> the initial Mach number is assumed to be 0.4 and is allowed to vary according to the one-dimensional compressible flow Mach-area relationship 

> [!quote|green]+ Highlight ([page. 27](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/Z37C5XV8?page=27&annotation=28D5UMWS))
> The noise source is assumed to be a planewave 

> [!quote|green]+ Highlight ([page. 27](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/Z37C5XV8?page=27&annotation=8W9PIXK4))
> The Downhill Simplex method [12] is used to determine the optimum impedance for the uniform case (one-zone) where the inner and outer wall impedances are identical 

> [!quote|green]+ Highlight ([page. 28](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/Z37C5XV8?page=28&annotation=M33KIWTV))
> The CDUCT code requires that the noise source be specified in terms of acoustic potential at the inlet of the duct. #CDL, #source-specification

> [!quote|green]+ Highlight ([page. 29](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/Z37C5XV8?page=29&annotation=ZT5MRJ8B))
> Morin [15] shows that broadband noise from a rotor/stator interaction is typically dominated by a noise source from the tip region. 

> [!quote|green]+ Highlight ([page. 30](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/Z37C5XV8?page=30&annotation=KGMIH2FH))
> the optimized impedances are not obtainable by current passive lining designs for the entire important frequency range
