### ✅ Supporting Argument or Example

> [!quote|green]+ Highlight ([page. 61](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/SKA9SW64?page=61&annotation=HZ442B3L))
> The aeroacoustic instability is caused by the constructive feedback between the acoustic modes of the cavity and the turbulent shear layer that forms at its opening. 

> [!quote|green]+ Highlight ([page. 61](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/SKA9SW64?page=61&annotation=B9DJP79A))
> Systematic experiments are performed in order to construct a new theoretical model, which describes the aeroacoustic system as two linearly stable oscillators, with linear reactive coupling, nonlinear damping and nonlinear resistive coupling. 

> [!quote|green]+ Highlight ([page. 62](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/SKA9SW64?page=62&annotation=26FYKLVT))
> The sound of flutes is produced through aeroacoustic instabilities that result from the constructive feedback between the acoustic modes of the instruments and the dynamics of a shear layer [54]. 

> [!quote|green]+ Highlight ([page. 62](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/SKA9SW64?page=62&annotation=NMMULCJ9))
> Most of the investigations considered the canonical problem of a single deep cavity, while some works deal with multiple deep cavities [40, 184] and liners made of deep cavities equipped with perforated plates [39]. 

> [!quote|green]+ Highlight ([page. 62](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/SKA9SW64?page=62&annotation=JGAQWEIV))
> There are also several studies dealing with various passive control methods to prevent whistling of a deep cavity, such as flow obstacles inside the cavity [113], internal cavity liner [72] or changes of the curvature of the cavity opening corners [188]. 

> [!quote|green]+ Highlight ([page. 63](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/SKA9SW64?page=63&annotation=2LSGUS5K))
> Many of the studies focusing on single deep cavities, including the present investigation, follow the work of Elder [50], who proposed a feedback loop analysis with the cavity opening and its aerodynamic forcing as a forward transfer function and the acoustic resonance of the deep cavity as a backward transfer function. 

> [!quote|green]+ Highlight ([page. 66](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/SKA9SW64?page=66&annotation=NWSDDNNB))
> In order to get insights in the aeroacoustic feedback at play in these self-sustained oscillations, Particle Image Velocimetry (PIV) is used to characterize the dynamics of the shear layer.
