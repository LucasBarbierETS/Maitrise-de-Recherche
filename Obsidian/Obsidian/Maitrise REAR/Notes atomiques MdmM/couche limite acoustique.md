
La couche limite visqueuse est un couche dans laquelle, pour un fluide visqueux, **même en condition du mur rigide, on considère des pertes visqueuses**

> [!quote|green]+ Highlight ([page. 16](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/DY74U56G?page=16&annotation=TXRW9TBM))
> According to this assumption, the viscous acoustic case in a hard duct is treated as an inviscid case in a duct of finite admittance at the wall. This admittance is introduced by and depends on the medium viscosity. 

Comme les équations sont plus simplement formalisées en milieu non visqueux, **on considère une admittance effective de la paroi qui tient compte de de ces effets visqueux proches de la parois**


> [!quote|green]+ Highlight ([page. 16](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/DY74U56G?page=16&annotation=QLYYWGDN))
> Th e acoustic boundary layer concept is based on the assumption that most viscosity effects exist in a thin region near the wall. Outside that region, the acoustic disturbance is mainly inviscid. 

> [!quote|green]+ Highlight ([page. 35](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/DY74U56G?page=35&annotation=9ACDGWC7))
> The acoustic boundary layer can be defined as the region near the wall where the longitudinal velocity changes very rapidly.
