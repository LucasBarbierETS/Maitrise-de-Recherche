

###### [[fischer2022]] 

- « the noise sources produced by the rotating blades can be decomposed into two main categories: inflow noise, which corresponds to the interaction between the incoming turbulent flow and the blades, and self noise, which is scattered by the unsteady pressure around the blades [3, 4]. » (Fischer et al., 2022, p. 2)

- « More particularly, self noise includes tip vortex noise from the blades [5] and trailing vortex sheet noise [6]. » (Fischer et al., 2022, p. 2)

- « For high Mach number flows, steady loading noise, created by the steady forces around the blade, and thickness noise, related to the displacement of fluid by a moving object, can become important. » (Fischer et al., 2022, p. 2)

- « In the case of a ducted rotor, the boundary layer developing along the inner duct wall can interact with the blade tip and create either broadband and/or tonal noise depending on the nature of the eddies present in the boundary layer [7]. » (Fischer et al., 2022, p. 2)
