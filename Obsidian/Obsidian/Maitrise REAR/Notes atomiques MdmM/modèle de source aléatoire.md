- « the source modal powers (and hence, amplitudes) and modal phases are allowed to vary randomly and independently. Thus, with equal probability of occurrence, the amplitude and phase for each cut-on source mode takes any value in the intervals from 0 to 1 and 0 to 2 pi, respectively. » (Nark et Jones, 2021, p. 444)

- « transmitted power appears to be normally distributed if the source includes all propagating circumferential and radial acoustic modes with random modal power and phase. » (Nark et Jones, 2021, p. 444)
